▸ HOW TO ADD VIDEOS
Scroll down in this file to the VIDEOS list near the bottom of the script.
For a long-form tutorial: add {id:'YOUTUBE_VIDEO_ID', title:'Video Title', meta:'12 min · Dashboard Basics'} to the longVideos array.
For a Short: add {id:'SHORT_VIDEO_ID', title:'Short Title'} to the shortVideos array.
The video ID is the part after v= in a normal YouTube URL, or after /shorts/ for a Short.
